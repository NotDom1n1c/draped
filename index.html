Draped

Five sheets of silk. Pull them away, one by one.

Every surface remembers

The hand that moved it.

Light is the only pattern

There is no print in the folds, only light.

Nothing stays folded

Sooner or later everything is pulled open.

Draped.

Five sheets, one shader, no photographs. A design study, 2026.
